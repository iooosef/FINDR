Honest CCian Guide

Found an Item on Campus?

Thank you for being honest!

To ensure items are safely returned to their rightful owners, students turn in found items directly to school authorities.

1
Bring the item to the Lost and Found Office

Located at Balbago Building, Ground Floor (near the Prefect / Guidance Office).

2
Hand it to the staff on duty

Inform the staff where and when you found the item.

3
Staff logs it into FINDR

The system immediately scans for registered missing reports.

4
Owner is notified

The matched student will be alerted to verify ownership in person.

Office Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
